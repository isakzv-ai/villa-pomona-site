From the castle to the island, the gorge to the lake — here's how to make the most of your stay at Villa Pomona.

Must-See

Top Attractions

The best of Bled — all within walking distance or a short drive from Villa Pomona.

🏰

Bled Castle

Perched on a cliff 130 metres above the lake, Bled Castle is one of Slovenia's oldest castles. Visit the museum, print workshop, wine cellar, and enjoy panoramic views of the lake and the Julian Alps.

🎫 ~€17 entry • 10-min walk from Villa Pomona

🚢

Bled Island & Church

Take a traditional pletna boat to the island, climb the 99 stone steps, ring the wishing bell, and explore the Church of the Assumption. A classic Bled experience.

🚢 Pletna boat ~€18 return • 3-min walk to boat station

🏔

Vintgar Gorge

A 1.6 km wooden walkway follows the Radovna River through a stunning limestone gorge with waterfalls, pools, and emerald water. One of the most beautiful walks in Slovenia.

🚶 ~€10 entry • 5-min drive from the villa

🌄

Lake Swimming

Grajsko beach is just steps from the villa — the main swimming spot with warm, clean water in summer. For a quieter spot, head to the west shore or Mala Zaka beach.

🌊 Free • Lifejackets at rental stands

🚴

Cycling Around the Lake

A 6 km path circles the entire lake — easy cycling with constant views. Bike rentals are available everywhere in town. Allow 30–40 minutes at a relaxed pace with photo stops.

🚲 Bike rental ~€10–15/hr • Flat easy terrain

🏌

Golf Course Bled

Just a short drive from Villa Pomona, the 18-hole Golf Course Bled (Golf Bled) offers stunning views of the Julian Alps and Lake Bled. A must for golf enthusiasts visiting the area.

🏌 18 holes • 10-min drive • Equipment rental available

🏂

Winter Sports

The lake shore turns into an outdoor ice skating rink in winter. Nearby Vogel ski resort offers skiing with lake views. Cross-country trails also available.

⛸ Vogel ski pass ~€45/day • 20-min drive

Explore Further

Day Trips

Venture beyond Bled — all easily reachable by car or with our shuttle service.

⛰

Lake Bohinj

Slovenia's largest permanent lake — quieter and wilder than Bled, with incredible swimming, hiking, and the Savica Waterfall nearby.

🚗 ~25 min drive • Free parking

🏰

Ljubljana

Slovenia's charming capital with the old town, Ljubljana Castle, Central Market, and the famous dragon bridge. Great for a half-day or evening trip.

🚗 ~40 min drive • Direct bus also available

🌃

Triglav National Park

Slovenia's only national park is on your doorstep. Hike to the Savica Waterfall, explore the Soča River valley, or challenge yourself with a climb to Mount Triglav.

🏕 Enter at Bohinj • Trails for all skill levels

🇹🇿

Venice, Italy

A stunning day trip across the border. We can arrange shuttle transport to Venice for a day of canals, culture, and Italian cuisine.

🚗 ~2.5 hr drive • Shuttle available on request

Active

Outdoor Adventures

For those who want to explore Bled's natural beauty up close.

🏑

Hiking Trails

From the easy lakeside path to the challenging climb up Mala Osojnica for the iconic island photo — there's a trail for every fitness level.

🚴

Stand-Up Paddleboarding

Rent a SUP and explore the lake from the water. Calm conditions make it perfect for beginners. Sunrise paddling is magical.

🌅

Paragliding

Soar above Bled with a tandem paragliding flight from nearby Straža hill. Unforgettable views of the lake, castle, and Alps.

Culinary

Food & Drink

🍴

Villa Pomona Breakfast

Start your day with breakfast by the pool or on the terrace — fresh pastries, local cheeses, seasonal fruits, and coffee with garden views.

Dinner at Villa Adora

Our sister property offers multi-course dinners prepared by a private chef. Exquisite Slovenian cuisine in an intimate setting.

🍷

Wine Tastings

🍰

Bled Cream Cake

Don't leave without trying the legendary kremšnita — Bled's iconic cream cake. The original is served at Hotel Park, just 5 min away.
